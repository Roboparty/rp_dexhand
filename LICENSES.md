| --- | --- | --- |

| Bundled rphand_ros at the same baseline | Its [package.xml](https://github.com/Roboparty/roboparty_dexhand/blob/eb923acf61a02febabc9db90ad4d5eed84ffbc39/ros2/rphand_ros/package.xml) declares `Proprietary` | Confirm the intended ROS/source distribution terms with the rights holder; do not assume a permissive or GPL grant |

| Vendor applications, protocol workbook, models and CAD | Original files retain applicable third-party terms | Check each package's notices and distribution scope |
