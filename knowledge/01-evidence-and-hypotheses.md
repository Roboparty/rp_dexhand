# Evidence and hypotheses

The implemented hypotheses concern algorithms on declared data generators. They do not concern the existence of Qi, consciousness reception or torsion transmission.

Let E denote mean contact-localization error, measured in millimeters. The pathway question is whether Δ = E_pathway − E_spatial is negative by a useful amount. The demonstration margin is 0.25 mm. A negative estimate smaller than that margin is not automatically practically useful.

For example, errors 3.0 and 3.1 mm give Δ = −0.1 mm: a numerical improvement, but below the configured margin. The benchmark requires the upper endpoint of its paired 95% interval to be below −0.25 mm before labeling the result promising on this generator.

This criterion is illustrative and was not obtained from hardware requirements. No prospective preregistration is claimed. The data generator and algorithm were developed together, so additional untouched conditions and real measurements are essential before a general claim.

**Why used:** an explicit endpoint prevents a collection of plausible diagrams from substituting for an experiment. A failed comparison can still establish that an implementation works as specified.
